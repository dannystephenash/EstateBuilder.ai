In Three Movements

Site, Massing, Report.

I.

Site Plan

Pick a Toronto-region parcel from Mapbox. Trace the lot, merge adjacent parcels, toggle zoning overlays, and read the by-law in context — all without leaving the browser.

Mapbox · Turf · ArcGIS overlays

II.

Massing

Generate residential mid-rise, multi-tower, or Class-A industrial massings. Step-backs, podium-tower transitions, angular planes, and shadow study — all live, with parametric controls.

Three.js r128 · custom geometry

III.

Reports

A branded PDF: cover, executive-summary KPI page, four-up 3D views, full pro-forma with right-aligned numerals and a sources-and-uses pie, plus zoning, comparables, and a DCF section.

jsPDF · brand-configurable · multi-page
